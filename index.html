Key Insights

What the data and model tell the retention team
  • Recency of last order is the standout signal. Churners go quiet 2.4x longer before leaving.
  • Tenure, spend and order volume barely differ between groups on their own.
  • Support tickets run 40% higher among churners.
  • Class weighting (not resampling) handled the 7.8% imbalance without synthetic data.
  • Operating point tuned to a 22% precision floor catches 61% of churners at a manageable false-alarm rate.
